## Scope
- In scope: CRM login, product create/update/terminate, order list and Pending payment filter, customer list/search controls.
- Out of scope: production CRUD, customer record changes, order payment/refund/cancellation, and checkout.

## Coverage notes
- Staging CRM: `https://www.stagingl10.storeberry.xyz/`. Login with the supplied Storeberry Flower company account succeeded and opened the CRM Get Started page.
- Products: created a hidden product named `ASIDE-CRM-QA-mvknd99k` (product ID 3195), category General, price HKD 1.00. First save without a category was rejected with the required-category alert. After selecting General from the category picker, creation succeeded and the row appeared in the active list. Updated the name to `ASIDE-CRM-QA-mvknd99k-UPDATED`; the list retained the updated name. Terminated the product using “Make product terminated in all platforms”. Active product count returned to 85 and terminated count increased to 83. The temporary product was not left active.
- Orders: last-12-month list loaded with 1,017 orders. Selecting Pending payment returned 204 orders. No order data was changed.
- Customers: list loaded with 320 records. Search controls for Member ID/Name/Mobile/Email and Remark were visible. No customer data was edited or included in this report.
- No CRM defect was confirmed in this limited pass. Per the request, production CRM was not tested because no CRM bug was found on staging.

## Knowledge updates
- CRM login is at `https://www.stagingl10.storeberry.xyz/auth/login` and requires company name, username and password.
- Product creation requires an existing category. The category picker is a multi-select; choose a listed category (e.g., General) from its options rather than typing free text.
- For safe product test data, select Hidden Product visibility and terminate the unique record after verification.
- Production CRM `https://www.mystoreberry.com/` remains untested. The CRM “Visit my online store” link on production led to `https://storeberry-flower.mystoreberry.com/`.
