## Goal
Staff can review product/brand comments left by customers.

## Preconditions
- Comments exist (or a QA comment is left on the eShop if the shop allows it)

## Steps
1. Open Brands > Brand Comment Review, switch region/language, sort, page through
2. Export

## Expected
- Comments match the selected brand/region, sorting and paging work
- The export contains the listed comments

## Notes
Feature with no cases before the 2026-10-29 feature-coverage review (menu/page found in the live staging app). Never tested.
