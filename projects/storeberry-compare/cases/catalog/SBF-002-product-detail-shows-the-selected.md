## Goal
A shopper can identify the selected item and its price before deciding to buy.

## Data
- A currently listed product with a visible price

## Steps
1. Open the storefront home page
2. Select a currently listed product
3. Review its product detail page

## Expected
- The detail page shows the selected product name
- The detail page shows a price for the selected product
